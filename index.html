How Ordering Works

Simple 3-step checkout with zero account login required.

  1. 01. Pick Views, Likes, or Both

    Select any tier from Instagram Views (₹3–₹110) and/or Instagram Likes (₹5–₹10). The combined payable amount updates automatically.

  2. 02. Paste a Public Instagram URL

    Copy the link from Instagram using Share → Copy Link. Make sure the account is public while delivery runs.

  3. 03. Scan QR, Enter UTR & Submit

    Pay the displayed ₹ amount via the UPI QR code, enter your Transaction ID / UTR number, and click Submit Order on WhatsApp.
